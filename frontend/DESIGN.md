# SKYFRAME OBSERVATORY frontend design

- **Product:** A public scientific viewer for comparing time-series SPHEREx observations, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.
- **Approved direction (November 2026):** High-tech celestial mission instrument, based on the team's amber-on-charcoal reference images and the approved celestial-navigator concept. No generated logo, fictional spacecraft dashboard or fake astronomy.
- **Primary job:** Pick a sky region in RA/Dec; load real SPHEREx observations; inspect their time variation and possible moving or variable candidates.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Text-only top navigation; compact controls at left; one central large canvas; evidence inspector at right; always-visible observation timeline at bottom. Navigator/Observations switch lives directly above the canvas.
- **Interactive 3D:** `CelestialNavigator.tsx` renders a real WebGL geometric equatorial sphere. Dragging rotates; scrolling zooms; clicking a visible point calculates J2000 RA and declination and updates existing input fields. Users explicitly choose when to load data. No starfield or measurements are generated by this globe.
- **Color and type:** Near-black `#06080a` and charcoal `#0b0f12`, restrained warm amber `#e4ab69` for actions, pale gray-white type. Normal system sans for UI, monospace only for sky coordinates and scientific measurements. Narrow dividers; no heavy rounded cards, hero slogans, blur, fake technical stats, or decorative metrics.
- **Navigation:** Explorer, Discover, Spectrum, Compare, Notebook, Guide; Settings opens/closes search controls. All existing views remain available.
- **Real data:** All observations, catalog sources, dates, flags, spectra and detected candidates come exclusively from the backend. A selected coordinate on the sphere is **not** an astronomical observation. Empty observations display `No data`.
- **Responsive:** At narrow widths the sidebar becomes a drawer; below 690px the inspector stacks under the viewer and the globe remains interactive.
- **Accessibility:** Standard form inputs provide a non-WebGL fallback; globe canvas supports arrow-key rotation and Enter/Space to choose the center of view. Respect reduced-motion settings, visible focus, and color contrast.
- **Data integration:** Only `src/services/api.ts` performs HTTP requests; the WebGL navigator never calls an external API. No backend code is owned or edited by this visual update.
