# Aladin Lite

The optional sky-atlas frame uses Aladin Lite 3.8.2, maintained by CDS,
Strasbourg. Its original license text is preserved at
`public/licenses/aladin-lite.txt` and is served with the application.

The package source is available at the following release commit.

[Aladin Lite source](https://github.com/cds-astro/aladin-lite/tree/3476c535e682336d1bde3dac98cf7db54fcc749f)

SPHEREx mosaic tiles are loaded from NASA/IPAC IRSA. The atlas displays a
multi-observation wavelength mosaic. Individual dated exposures continue to
provide the app's time sequence.
